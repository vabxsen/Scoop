What Scoop runs


            

              Scoop shows you the plan before anything is saved — and the custom command field
              lets you add your own yt-dlp arguments on top.
            

          

        


        

          

            

              
01

              
Image collections, without recompression

              

                Preview a public page or gallery, select individual images or save the complete
                set. Direct image links keep their original bytes and format.
              

            

            
          


          

            
02

            
Video and audio, configured per download

            
Choose quality, container, audio format, subtitles and thumbnail options.

          


          

            
03

            
A queue that keeps moving

            
Track progress, retry failures and manage completed files from one history.
